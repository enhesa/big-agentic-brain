Postbird Support - ticket #88213 "Promo campaign accepted (202) but not delivered"

Postbird (Jonas): Hi! Your account sends everything on the default "transactional" stream. Since
2026-09-15, bulk and marketing mail sent on the transactional stream is accepted with 202 and then
silently dropped. Set the header

    X-Postbird-Stream: broadcast

on every marketing, newsletter or promo message. Broadcast messages must also carry one-click
unsubscribe headers or they are dropped as well:

    List-Unsubscribe: <mailto:...>, <https://...>       (both a mailto: and an https: URI)
    List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click

Transactional mail (password resets, receipts) needs no change.
